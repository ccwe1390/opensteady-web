Help the user record a compatibility check of `$ARGUMENTS`.

- Never visit or test sites that hold money, health records, messages or accounts with real consequences. Suggest a harmless page instead.
- Walk through the checklist in `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md`: enable on the tab, outline visible on links and buttons, near miss on a link, quick drag guard on a link, repeat guard, Escape, forms left native, risky labels excluded, page performance, and turning it off.
- Ask the user for each observation. Don't invent outcomes, and don't drive a logged-in browser.
- Append a dated row: site (hostname only), Chrome version, OS, each check with pass, fail or not tried, and notes. Don't record private URLs, account names or screenshots of personal data.
